## Summary

- The app is a static browser app. It does not provide accounts, server-side project storage, or project uploads.
- Imported images, fonts, templates, colors, and edit states are handled in the user's browser memory, browser storage, or downloaded files.
- The app includes Google Analytics scripts and Google Fonts loading, so those external services may process technical access information under their own policies.
- Browser-local data is used for editing, template reuse, color management, export, and restoring work.
- Users can delete browser-local data from app controls or browser site-data controls.
